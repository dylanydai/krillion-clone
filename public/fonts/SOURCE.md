# Nimbus Sans L

The regular and bold TrueType files are unmodified copies from
[Font Library](https://fontlibrary.org/en/font/nimbus-sans-l).

Download: https://fontlibrary.org/assets/downloads/nimbus-sans-l/2c1b22cb6708de61d2051f12c90a024e/nimbus-sans-l.zip

The original license and notices are included in `COPYING` and `README`.

## VT323

The unmodified VT323 pixel font comes from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/vt323).
Its SIL Open Font License is included in `VT323-OFL.txt`.
